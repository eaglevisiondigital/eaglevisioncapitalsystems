# Eagle Vision V64 — Prime49 Combined Bottom-Line Calculator

## Calculator changes
- Retains the Prime49 payout estimate of $20 per $5,000 in qualifying monthly card volume.
- Retains the $15,000 minimum and $5,000 volume increments.
- Adds a merchant-selectable estimated current all-in effective payment cost rate from 2.9% to 5.0%, defaulting to 4.5%.
- Estimates monthly and annual payment costs potentially eliminated under an approved Prime49 setup.
- Separately displays:
  1. Prime49 monthly and annual payout.
  2. Estimated monthly and annual payment costs eliminated.
  3. Combined monthly and annual bottom-line impact.
- Passes all calculator assumptions and results into the Prime49 review form.
- Adds explicit illustrative-estimate language and requires Eagle Vision statement review before final terms are confirmed.

## Formula
- Monthly payout = monthly card volume / $5,000 × $20.
- Annual payout = monthly payout × 12.
- Monthly estimated costs eliminated = monthly card volume × selected all-in effective rate.
- Annual estimated costs eliminated = monthly estimated costs eliminated × 12.
- Combined impact = payout + estimated costs eliminated.
