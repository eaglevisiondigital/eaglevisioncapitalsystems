Prime49 FAQ

Questions merchants ask before the walkthrough.

The walkthrough exists to make the program specific to your business rather than forcing every merchant into the same setup.

Will customers see anything different?+

Yes. Pricing is shown clearly at checkout. You review and approve the presentation before anything is activated.

How much will the monthly payout be?+

The current Prime49 estimate is $20 per month for every $5,000 in qualifying card volume. At the $15,000 program minimum, that is $60 per month or $720 per year. Eligibility and final program terms are confirmed during the Eagle Vision walkthrough.

Will I need to replace my current equipment?+

Not always. Compatibility depends on the existing environment. Eagle Vision confirms what can stay, what needs to change and which equipment fits best.

Is every business eligible?+

No. Eligibility varies by business type, processing profile and operational complexity. Seasonal and complex environments may fit a different Eagle Vision payment strategy.

What about compliance?+

The program is structured around applicable card-brand rules and state law, with clear checkout presentation and full terms and disclosures during enrollment.

Can Prime49 support multiple locations?+

Yes, subject to fit. Multi-location and multi-station requirements are mapped during enrollment so the package reflects how the organization operates.

Is there a long-term contract?+

Current Prime49 program materials state there is no long-term Prime49 contract. Review all final terms and disclosures before enrollment.
